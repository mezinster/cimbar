# Support

Write to [support@nfcarchiver.com](mailto:support@nfcarchiver.com), or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/mezinster/cimbar/issues).

## What to include

So that we can reproduce the problem, please tell us:

- which client you used: the web app (and which browser), the Android app or the iPhone app;
- your device model and its system version;
- the app version, shown in the app's **About** tab (in the web app, at the bottom of **How it works**);
- whether you were sending or receiving, and how (Present, GIF, live scan, photo);
- what you did, and what happened instead of what you expected.

Please do not send the contents of your files or any passphrases.
