# Privacy Policy

The CimBar privacy policy, covering the web app, the Android app and the iPhone/iPad app, is published at:

**https://nfcarchiver.com/cimbar/docs/privacy/**

It is also available in Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish and Georgian from the same page. Its source is [`site-docs/docs/en/privacy.md`](site-docs/docs/en/privacy.md), and its history is the history of that file.

In short: CimBar collects nothing. Encoding, decoding, compression and encryption happen entirely on your device, and your files, messages and passphrases are never sent to any server.

Questions: support@nfcarchiver.com.
